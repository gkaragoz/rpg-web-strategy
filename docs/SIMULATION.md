# Simulation

- `src/sim/battle.ts` owns the step loop and the persistent state (the `Battle` class); the rest of the combat code is split into function modules that take the battle as first argument: `battleConfig.ts` (config, constants, capacities, `terrainFor`), `battleAi.ts` (threat, kiting, chase, leash, target choice), `battleSkills.ts` (attacks, skills, projectile landing), `battleDamage.ts` (damage, death, zones, comedy physics), `battleEffects.ts` (status effects, gathering), `battleSetup.ts` (spawn layouts). Members the modules need are marked `@internal`. It owns the step loop: targeting, movement on the grid (`grid.ts`), attacks, skills, projectiles (`projectiles.ts`), terrain effects (`terrain.ts`).
- Unit state is struct-of-arrays in `units.ts` (typed arrays) for speed; stats are normalized from the raw roster numbers there.
- **Determinism**: same seed + same inputs ⇒ same state on every client. Randomness only via `Mulberry` (`random.ts`). The state hash is compared every 60 steps online.
- `snapshot.ts` serializes/restores a battle (used for late joiners and desync recovery); new state must be included in `serialize/restore/hash`.
- Stall detector (`testing/stall.ts`) and fuzz tests guard against fights that never end.
- Rules for editing: `.claude/rules/sim-determinism.md`.

## Map guarantees (`Terrain.fixPockets`, `mapsanity.test.ts`)
Every generated map is playable: no walkable tile is unreachable from the main area. Closed-in ground (a plateau or valley ringed by cliffs, an island) gets a ramp when one fits (a gradual slope cut into the higher side; the tiles beside it that end up too high become cliffs), otherwise it is sealed as an obstacle and dressed with rocks (`dressSealed`), e.g. the ridge walls of the valley layout. Everything is applied to the point-mirrored tile too, so maps stay fair, and it only depends on the seed, so all online clients build the same map. The sanity test sweeps all presets, layouts, reliefs and rivers over sizes and seeds: no unreachable walkable ground, point symmetry, bounded sealed ground, roomy team zones for 2/4/6 teams.


## Game modes (`src/sim/modes/`)
`BattleConfig.rules` (`GameModeId`: `classic | hill | vip | ctf | siege | horde | lanes`, default `classic`; not to be confused with `BattleConfig.mode`, the launch kind) selects a `ModeRules` instance (`modeFor(id)`, one per `Battle`). All modes are autonomous: nothing is ordered during a battle.
- Hooks: `init(b)` at the end of `Battle.reset`, `step(b, dt)` once per step after the normal logic while the battle is undecided, `winner(b)` (team, -2 draw, undefined: go on) after the last-team-alive check, `serialize()/restore()` (plain numbers, stored in `snapshot.extra.mode`), `hud(b)` (pure `ModeHud` data for the UI) and `scores()`. Non-classic mode state and the team goals are part of `Battle.hash`; classic hashes are unchanged (`golden.test.ts`). The `checkDraw` no-damage draws only apply in classic.
- **Goal hook**: `Battle.teamGoalX/Y/teamHasGoal` (per team, serialized). `marchToEnemy` (units without a target) heads for the team goal when set, otherwise for the nearest enemy centroid as before. Fighting and target choice are unchanged.
- **King of the Hill** (`hill.ts`): 3 control points (map centre plus two flanks, ~28% of the map size from the centre perpendicular to the spawn axis; always on walkable tiles of the main region, see `hillControlPoints`), capture radius `max(3.5, size * 0.06)`. Every 10 steps the owner of a point is the team with strictly the most living units inside it (equal top counts: contested, -2; nobody inside: the owner keeps it). Each owned point gives +1 point per second, counted in whole steps (no float drift). First to 100 points wins (a tie at the top continues), after 600 s the leader wins (tie: draw). Each team's goal is the nearest point it does not own. `Battle.modeHud()` / `modeScores()` expose the display data.
- **VIP** (`vip.ts`): every team has one VIP unit. When it dies (any path through `killUnit`, via the optional `ModeRules.onKill` hook) its whole team dies at once through the normal kill path (counters, `death` events and meter kill credit go to the killer of the VIP); the usual last-team-standing logic then decides. The classic no-damage draws are bypassed; after 600 s the team whose VIP has the highest hp fraction wins (tie: draw, -2). Which unit: `BattleConfig.vips[team]` is a **layout index** (k-th group of 4 floats of `config.layout`) or -1 for auto (highest max HP, ties: lowest unit index; summon-pool units are never picked). `spawnLayout` skips entries whose spot has no walkable tile, so layout index != unit index: `Battle.layoutUnits[layoutIndex]` (-1 when skipped) maps it at `reset`. An out-of-range, skipped, foreign-team or non-integer pick falls back to auto; demo/bench layouts always auto-pick. State (VIP unit index and an out flag per team) goes into `serialize/restore/hash`. `Battle.vipUnits()` (Int32Array, length MAX_TEAMS, -1 none) and `vipUnitsOf(modeHud)` give the unit indices to a renderer; `ModeHud` is `{ kind: 'vip'; teams: [{ unit, hp (0..1), alive }] x MAX_TEAMS; timeLeft }`. The worker frame carries it in `F_MODE_ON = 2` + `F_VIP_BASE` (hill layout unchanged). No scores. The offline studio builds `config.vips` from its `PlacedUnit.vip` flags (layout order = placement order).
- **Capture the Flag** (`ctf.ts`): each team has one flag at a home base = centroid of its initial (alive, non-pool) units snapped to the nearest walkable tile of the main region (`ctfBasePoint`), base radius `ctfRadius(size) = max(3, size * 0.05)`. Every 5 steps (steps 1, 6, ...) units are scanned in index order: the first living, non-summoned unit of another team within the radius of a flag at home becomes its carrier (lowest index wins ties; a unit carries one flag; pool/dormant/summoned units never carry). New optional hook `ModeRules.drive(b, i, dt)` (called from `Battle.updateUnit` after the stun/fear checks, only for modes that define it): a carrier ignores the fight and `queueMove`s to its own base at normal speed. Every step a carrier inside its own base radius scores (`captures` +1), the flag returns home and the unit is released; a dead carrier (`onKill`) returns the flag at once. Goals (re-evaluated every 5 steps): a team with a carrier heads for its own base (escort), else if its flag is carried it hunts the carrier's current position, else the nearest enemy flag at home. First to 3 captures wins (a tie at the top continues); after 600 s the most captures win (tie or all zero: draw, -2); last team standing wins as usual; the classic no-damage draws are bypassed. State (captures, carrier per flag, base x/y/exists, radius) is in `serialize/restore/hash`. `scores()` = captures per team.
  `ModeHud` ctf: `{ kind: 'ctf'; captures: number[] /* MAX_TEAMS */; target: 3; radius: number; flags: Array<{ team: number /* flag owner */; x; y; carrier: number /* unit index or -1 */; carrierTeam: number /* -1 at home */ }> /* one per team with units, team order; x,y = base at home, else the carrier's current position */; timeLeft }`. Worker frame: `F_MODE_ON = 3`, captures in `F_SCORE_BASE`, 5 values per team from `F_CTF_BASE` (owner team or -1, x, y, carrier, carrierTeam), `F_CTF_RADIUS`; `F_LEN` was 105 (hill/vip layouts unchanged; siege extends it to 111).
- **Siege** (`siege.ts`): team `BattleConfig.siegeDefender` (integer 0..teams-1, anything else: team 0) defends a **core**, every other team attacks. The core is a *structure unit* (kind `siege-core`, `SIEGE_CORE_KIND`, 3000 HP, armor 0.2, scale 2) spawned at the end of `Battle.reset` by `Battle.spawnStructure`. Position (`siegeCorePoint`): the map's `keep` decor (citadel layout) snapped to the nearest walkable main-region tile, else the centroid of the defender's initial units snapped the same way (`ctfBasePoint`). Attackers win by destroying the core: the winner is the team of the unit that dealt the final blow (`onKill`); if the killer is unknown (damage over time, environment) or a defender, the attacker team with the most damage on the core (`ModeRules.onDamage`, ties: lowest team index; no damage at all: draw -2). The defender wins when 300 s of sim time pass with the core alive, and when every attacker is dead (last team standing). Decision: the defender's *army* dying does NOT end the battle, because the core keeps the defender "alive" for the last-team-standing check (`Battle.alivePerTeam + structPerTeam`); attackers must destroy the core. The classic no-damage draws are bypassed. Goals (every 5 steps): every living team's units without a target head for the core, so attackers advance and defenders gather at it. State (defender, core unit, core x/y, credited winner, damage per attacking team) is in `serialize/restore/hash`; `scores()` is undefined.
  `ModeHud` siege: `{ kind: 'siege'; defender; coreHp /* 0..1, 0 when destroyed */; coreMaxHp; coreUnit /* stable unit index, also after death; -1 only if no slot */; x; y; timeLeft }`. Worker frame: `F_MODE_ON = 4`, six values from `F_SIEGE_BASE` (105), `F_LEN` is 111.
  **Structure units** (reusable by later modes): `TroopDef.structure` / `KindStats.structure` (`isStructure(kind)`, `STRUCT_OF`), role `Role.Structure`, always also `summoned` so every placement/gallery/economy/balance filter hides it (`buyable` is false, the host answers `unbuyable`). Speed 0, power 0, no skill, user stat overrides are ignored. A structure is a normal unit slot (snapshots and the hash cover it) but `Battle.updateStructure` replaces `updateUnit`: no AI/targeting (`Picker` skips it), no movement or separation, ignores knockback, launch (comedy), stun, fear and taunt (guards in `applyStun/applyFear/applyTaunt/pushUnit/launch/areaStrike`), tanks do not push it; it is a valid enemy target (melee gets `STRUCT_REACH` extra range because the body is big), it keeps its team "present" for the enemy lists and the winner check but is not counted in `Battle.alivePerTeam` (what players see), the army centroid or the damager count; `Battle.structPerTeam` counts them. Capacity for a mode's structures is reserved in `unitCapacity` only for that mode (siege: +1), so other modes keep their buffers. `structureKinds(rules)` lists the kinds a mode spawns so the atlas can bake them.
- **Horde** (`horde.ts`, `hordeWaves.ts`): wave defence with **alliances**. Every human team is an ally against one AI team, the horde, whose team index is the number of human teams (`hordeTeamIndex(config)`; `BattleConfig.teamCount` stays the human count, 1..5 humans, `humanTeamCount` clamps it; `totalTeamCount` = humans + 1, at most `MAX_TEAMS`). `ModeRules.lastStanding = false`: the mode alone decides the result (the built-in last-team-standing rule is off, and so are the classic draws).
  *Alliances*: `teamGroups(config)` gives `group[team]` (`Battle.group`, default `group[t] = t`, so every other mode is byte-identical and `golden.json` is untouched). Horde: all human teams are group 0, the horde its own group. Every enemy lookup goes through `Battle.enemyList` (rebuilt each step; teams of the same group are skipped), `Picker.prepare` (same rule, the helper threads get the groups from `teamGroups(config)`), `nearestEnemy` and `pushAroundTanks`, so targeting, projectiles/splash/lines/zones, kiting threat scans, tank strikes and pushes, melee, marching and the win checks all treat allies like the own team. The hex-links setup (`destinyBond`) also skips allies. Healing: `Battle.selfList[team]` is now "all teams of the group" (classic: just the team), so priests, the nun's area and `healArea` heal allied teams too; separation and pathing never looked at teams, so allies block each other exactly like own-team units. Meter and `alivePerTeam` are indexed by team as before (the horde team is index `humans`).
  *Waves*: the horde is a pool of dormant units (alive 0, `deathT` 1000, like the bone-caller skeletons) spawned at the end of `Battle.reset` (`init`), capacity reserved only for horde rules in `unitCapacity` (`hordePoolSize(humans)`, identical on every client). Wave n (1..12) has `(4 + 3(n-1)) * humans` units (246 per human team in total) and wakes at step `(15 + 25 (n-1)) * stepsPerSecond` (integer step counting: 15 s, 40 s, ..., 290 s) at the spawn gates; units of one wave are consecutive in the pool, unit k uses gate `k mod gates` plus a hash jitter (`hordeHash(wave, k)`) snapped to a walkable main-region tile within 3 tiles. Kinds are a pure function (`hordeKind(n, k, humans)`): tier by stratification from `HORDE_MIX` (percent common/rare/epic/legendary per wave: waves 1-3 all common, rare from 4, epic from 7, legendary from 10), kind inside a tier = `hordeHash % list`, roster `HORDE_ROSTER` = placeable, non-structure, non-summoned, without `summonSkeleton`/`destinyBond` (those need placement-time setup). Each wave also restarts the stale-damage clock (`lastDeathTime`). Horde units use the classic AI: no target -> march to the nearest enemy centroid; humans without a target march to the horde.
  *Gates*: up to 3 (`HORDE_GATES`) perimeter points (12 candidates per map side, inset 2.5 tiles, snapped with `nearestWalkable(.., mainOnly)`), ranked by their distance to the nearest human centroid; the best one first, then points at least 0.2 x map size apart and with at least 75% of the best score. They are saved in the mode state.
  *Result*: victory = wave 12 has spawned and no horde unit lives -> winner = lowest-index human team with living units; defeat = every human unit dead -> winner = horde team index; 900 s of sim time with the horde alive -> defeat (never a draw). Spawning stops when no human is alive. `scores()` = `[waves reached for each human team ..., 0 for the horde]` (allies: equal values; waves reached = waves spawned while any human lived = "waves survived" incl. the one that killed them; 12 on a win). State: wave counter, pool start, gate count, gate x/y (`serialize/restore/hash`; the pool lives in the unit arrays).
  `ModeHud` horde: `{ kind: 'horde'; wave /* spawned so far */; waves: 12; nextIn /* s, 0 when none left */; hordeAlive; hordeTeam; timeLeft }`. Worker frame: `F_MODE_ON = 5`, five values from `F_HORDE_BASE` (111), `F_LEN` is 116. UI contract: teams in a horde battle are `0..humans-1` (humans) and `humans` (horde): colour/arrays must have `humans + 1` entries (at most 6 = `MAX_TEAMS`); `GameInit.teams` / `BattleConfig.teamCount` stay `humans`.
  *Horde balance* (constants at the top of `hordeWaves.ts`; tooling `src/sim/testing/hordeBalance.ts`: `hordeScenario(seed, humans, gold)` builds seeded gold-equal armies of 3-5 random kinds on a random map, `runHorde` plays it): a typical 2000-gold army reaches on average 8.2 waves (seeds 1-40, range 6-11; i.e. it survives about 7 full waves), two teams 7.9, three 7.6, five 7.7 (10 seeds each); 4000 gold ~10.0, 6000 gold ~11.4 (one win in ten). Waves scale with the number of human teams, so adding allies does not trivialise them.
- **Lane Conquest** (`lanes.ts`, `lanesWaves.ts`, `battleTower.ts`, `terrainLanes.ts`): Three-lane push mode, exactly **two teams** (`humanTeamCount` is 2 for these rules; layout units of other teams are ignored), one **castle** each, three lanes, towers and waves of lane soldiers. `lastStanding = false` (a team without an army keeps getting waves; the classic draws are off).
  *Map*: with `rules === 'lanes'` `terrainFor` ignores the configured recipe except its biome (`lanesSpec`, `effectiveMapSpec(config)`; the configured seed still varies the map) and uses the dedicated layout `'lanes'` (`LayoutType`, **not** in `LAYOUTS`/presets/`randomSpec`/`sanitizeSpec`, so nothing else changed; `golden`, `mapaudit` and the map suites are untouched). `mapSizeFor` clamps the size up to `LANES_MIN_SIZE` = 80. Generation (`terrainLanes.ts`, a branch at the top of the `Terrain` constructor, no river/relief/villages/random forest): two rounded plazas (x < 0.22 n and x > 0.78 n, y from 0.12 n to 0.88 n) and three corridors 6-7 tiles wide (centre lines y = 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8 n; the outer two wave gently from the seed, mirrored, the middle one is straight) that run end to end; everything else is blocked (a forest wall thick near the lanes, sparse deeper in, with rocks), the lane centres carry a dirt road. The map is mirror-symmetric (x and y). `Terrain.laneOf` / `laneAt(x, y)` give the lane (0..2) of a corridor tile (-1 in a plaza, on a wall, and on every other layout). `Terrain.laneGeo` (`computeLanesGeometry`, a pure function of the finished map) holds the castle (x = 0.10 n / 0.90 n, y = n/2, snapped to walkable main-region ground), 8 waypoints per lane (the centre of the walkable run of the corridor at 8 evenly spaced columns, never assumed), a spawn point per team and lane (4.5 tiles in front of the castle towards the lane) and the tower spots (x = 0.30 n and 0.42 n from the team's own edge, lane centre). All positions are walkable and in the main region; all ground is reachable (no pockets, no ramps, nothing sealed). The walls are 17+ tiles thick at n = 80, so no range crosses them; the way from one lane to another leads through a plaza (about 2.9 times the straight distance between neighbouring lanes at mid map). To keep units in their lane the picker never picks a target in another lane: a unit's lane is `Units.lane` (the lane a soldier was released into, kept on the plazas) or else `Terrain.laneAt` of its position; units outside lanes (humans on a plaza, castles) can be picked by anyone. `Battle.noLeash` switches off the army leash and the army-pull of retreating archers (lanes are separate fronts). Placement zones (`teamZones` -> `laneZones` in `net/economy.ts`, chosen by `terrain.layout === 'lanes'`): team 0 = the left half (x from 2 to n/2 - 3), team 1 = the right half, so the human armies stand around their own castle plaza and lane starts.
  *Castle* (`lane-castle`, `LANE_CASTLE_KIND`, structure, 5000 HP, armor 0.2, heavy armor class, scale 2.4) and *tower* (`lane-tower`, `LANE_TOWER_KIND`, 900 HP, armor 0.2, heavy class, scale 1.7): both appended after `siege-core` (older kind numbers never change), `structure` + `summoned` (unbuyable, hidden everywhere, immune to knockback/launch/stun/fear/taunt, not counted in `alivePerTeam`). The tower is the first **armed structure** (`TroopDef.ranged` on a structure keeps its attack numbers: range 9, 28 pierce damage, cooldown 1.2 s): `Battle.updateStructure` calls `towerStep` (`battleTower.ts`); when its cooldown is ready it scans the enemy team grids (allocation-free, nearest wins, a `TOWER_SCAN` = 0.2 s pause when nothing is in range) and shoots with `fireShot` through the normal projectile pipeline (arrow; it lands on the nearest enemy near the aim point). Towers target only living, non-structure units (`towerTarget`). The range is 9 (a little above "8") because an archer (range 9) parks at 0.95 x 9 and would otherwise out-range a tower for free. Enemies pick towers and castles like any unit (melee gets `STRUCT_REACH`). 2 towers per lane per team, 6 per team (12 in all); with the 2 castles these are the 14 structure slots `unitCapacity` reserves (`LANES_STRUCTURES`).
  *Waves* (`lanesWaves.ts`, constants block at the top): the first wave comes at 10 s, then every 25 s (integer step counting; 29 waves fit the 720 s limit). Every castle releases the wave of every lane from a **dormant pool** reserved at reset (`lanesPoolSize()` = 1392 soldiers; `unitCapacity` adds 14 + 1392 for these rules only, identical on every client; the pool units are spawned at the end of `reset`, alive 0, `Units.lane` set). Composition per lane (`LANES_COMP`): 3 warriors (+1 every 10 waves) + 2 archers (+1 every 15 waves), a Shield Tank from wave 5, a Hammer Tank (`Kind.Brute`) from wave 10, i.e. 15 soldiers per team in wave 1 (5 per lane). Pool index of soldier k of lane l of team t in wave n = `lanesPoolIndex`; each soldier appears around its spawn point with a hash jitter; every wave restarts the stale-damage clock. Soldiers walk the lane with **per-unit goals**: `Battle.unitGoalX/unitGoalY/unitHasGoal` (typed arrays sized for the capacity only in lanes battles, empty otherwise; in the hash; rebuilt from the mode state in `restore`), set by the mode to the soldier's next waypoint (reached within 2 tiles, checked every 4 steps; after the last waypoint the goal is the enemy castle). `marchToEnemy` uses the unit goal first (before the leash, the team goal and the nearest enemy) for units WITHOUT a target, so soldiers attack what they meet (enemies, towers) and then continue. Human-placed armies get no goal (classic AI, A* through the lanes).
  *Result*: destroying the enemy castle wins for the other team (with two teams the killer is always the other team; both castles falling in one step is a draw, -2); at 720 s the castle with the higher health fraction wins, equal fractions draw (-2). `scores()` is undefined. State (waves released, pool start, castle units, tower units, waypoint index per pool soldier) is in `serialize/restore/hash`.
  `ModeHud` lanes: `{ kind: 'lanes'; castles: Array<{ team; hp /* 0..1 */; maxHp; unit; x; y }> /* length 2, by team */; towers: number[] /* living per team, length 2 */; wave /* released */; nextIn /* s, 0 after the last */; timeLeft }`. Worker frame: `F_MODE_ON = 6`, 14 values from `F_LANES_BASE` (116: per team hp, maxHp, unit, x, y; then towers x2, wave, nextIn), `F_LEN` is 130. `structureKinds('lanes')` = castle, tower, Warrior, Archer, Shield, Brute (the app bakes them into the atlas). A lanes battle has `pool + 14 + placed` unit slots (`unitCapacity`).
  *Balance (empty armies, size 80, seeds 1-3)*: the waves clash in the middle of each lane, the winner of a clash pushes on to the next tower and later waves reinforce the push; towers start to fall at about 2-5 min and 6-12 of the 12 are down by the end, one match ended at 698 s with a destroyed castle, the others at 720 s by castle HP fraction (e.g. 0.67 against 1.00). A symmetric empty match is therefore decided by a push or, otherwise, by the HP-fraction fallback; an exact tie (both castles untouched) is a draw. About 5 s of wall time per 12-minute match at ~200 soldiers; 3000 human units plus waves stay far below the 250 ms/step bound asserted in `modeslanes.test.ts`.
